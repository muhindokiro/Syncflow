About us

Enterprise ERP discipline, Odoo speed.

SyncFlow Group is an Odoo consulting and development company helping businesses across East and Central Africa run on modern, compliant ERP. Formerly ServiceScope Systems, we are now an official Odoo partner.

We are built and led by delivery professionals with enterprise ERP pedigree (SAP Business One and S/4HANA) and formal program-management credentials: PgMP, PMP and PRINCE2 Agile.

Why Odoo, why now

The compliance wave. Real-time e-invoicing is now mandatory across the region, and every business needs ERP that is compliant by design.

Regional Odoo Enterprise ≈ $19–24 per user per month, billed yearly. Verify current rates at odoo.com/pricing.
